# Climate Color
> Find the kind of climate action that energizes you.

---

## 🎨 About the Project
Climate Color helps people find their **climate color**: the kind of climate action that gives them energy. A short quiz points each person to three colors. Each color has an archetype, like the Connector or the Keeper, and the three together make a **blend** with its own nature name, like Lichen or Aster. From there, people can explore ideas, resources, and real stories that match their colors.

It also gives groups a shared language, so a team can see what each person brings and where it could use more help.

* **Creator:** Heather Succio

## 🧩 What's Inside

* **The Quiz:** 40 short activities, each with one question: "Would you enjoy this?" Each color is scored on its own, and the top three make your result.
* **8 Colors, 8 Archetypes:** Yellow the Visionary, Orange the Amplifier, Red the Catalyst, Violet the Guardian, Indigo the Keeper, Purple the Connector, Green the Anchor, and Blue the Architect.
* **56 Blends:** every mix of three colors, each named for something in nature.
* **Pathways:** a page for each color, with ways to put it to work.
* **The Directory:** curated guides, grants, and tools, sorted by color.
* **The Story Map:** real stories from around the world of people making each color's move.

---

## 🛠️ How It's Built

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no build step. Everything runs in the browser. There are no accounts and no tracking, and the public quiz never records answers. (Pages do load fonts from Google Fonts.)

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `core/` | The color system, in one copy every page reads: colors, archetypes, blends, and the wheel. `core/index.html` shows it all. |
| `quiz/` | The quiz and results page |
| `pathways/` | The wheel and one page per color |
| `directory/` | The resource directory |
| `storymap/` | The Story Map |
| `tools/` | A scoring simulator and the Squarespace embed helper |

To change a color, archetype, or blend, edit `core/`, and every page follows.

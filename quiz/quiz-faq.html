Quiz FAQ

answers to a few common questions about how the quiz works

← Back to Quiz
Does the quiz collect personal data?

No. The quiz operates entirely within the Climate Color website as a lightweight self-assessment. You can complete it completely anonymously without creating an account or submitting identifying information.

Are my answers tracked or sent to a server?

No. Your responses are processed locally in your browser. We never track your answers, send them to remote servers, or build a user profile.

Will I lose my results if I leave the page?

No. Your browser temporarily holds your final style result so you can freely explore the rest of the site and return anytime without losing your work.

Will my email or contact info be shared?

No. Taking the quiz does not require an email address, and you can bookmark or copy a direct link to your results freely. If you voluntarily opt to receive follow-up resources in the future, your information is kept secure and will never be sold or shared.

Is the web application secure to use?

Yes. The app uses standard HTTPS encryption, ensuring your connection to the website remains private and secure.

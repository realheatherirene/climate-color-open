Climate Color Pathways

Eight ways people naturally contribute to climate care.

Explore Your Path

Each Pathway represents a distinct way of engaging with climate work — through momentum, energy, imagination, steadiness, structure, memory, relationship, or protection. These are not roles or identities. They are invitations.

  • Driver — The Path of Momentum
  • Advocate — The Path of Energy
  • Visionary — The Path of Imagination
  • Stabilizer — The Path of Steadiness
  • Architect — The Path of Structure
  • Keeper — The Path of Memory
  • Connector — The Path of Relationship
  • Guardian — The Path of Protection

You don’t need to fit into one Pathway. You don’t need to choose. You don’t need to stay the same. These are simply ways of seeing yourself with gentleness and clarity.

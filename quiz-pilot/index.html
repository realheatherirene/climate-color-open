Before you start

This is a pilot version of the Climate Color Quiz. Unlike the public quiz — which never records your answers — this version saves your answers, how long each one took, and your result, so we can check whether the quiz is working the way it's meant to.

Afterward, we'll ask a few optional questions about whether your result felt accurate and a little about your background. We don't ask for your name or email.

Your responses are stored privately by Climate Color, used only to improve the quiz, never shared publicly, and deleted within 90 days after the pilot ends. If you stop partway through, the answers you've already given are kept and used the same way.

Taking part is completely optional, and you can take the regular, untracked quiz instead.
